# VoltMind 100-kVA deployment model card

Underlying artifact: `model/100kVA_model.joblib`; model version **VoltMind_100kVA_Final_Selected_v3**; status **FINAL_SELECTED_SYNTHETIC_BENCHMARK_LOCKED**. Deployment: **VoltMind_100kVA_Hackathon_Runtime_v1.0**. The classifier was not trained, retrained or fine-tuned, and its weights, preprocessing, features and cutoff were not modified. SHA256 is recorded in config, audit and final manifest; startup verifies model bytes before loading. Original model card and reproducibility record are preserved in provenance.

Purpose: observe thermal excess using causal actual-minus-expected top-oil residuals for a 100-kVA three-phase distribution transformer; provide separate electrical operating screens and data quality to the Digital Twin. Original context: synthetic 100-kVA, 11kV/433V, 50Hz, Dyn11, ONAN. The artifact's original training used R00–R05 and validation R06/R07; U/S traces subsequently became threshold development. The original untouched N/T acceptance used 24 replays and 96,768 rows with independently generated separate synthetic commissioning. No new model selection occurred in this deployment.

Architecture: packaged train-only median SimpleImputer → StandardScaler → LogisticRegression(C=1, solver=lbfgs, max_iter=2000, random_state=100). Frozen feature order: residual_C, residual_mean_30min_C, residual_mean_60min_C, residual_change_30min_C. Means use six/twelve trailing samples with original minimum counts; change compares six samples back. The thermal recurrence uses previous equilibrium, ambient, mean squared normalized phase currents, and separate rise/tau/bias/loss fraction. No future samples, oracle labels, events, profiles, asset identity or filenames enter the estimator.

Decision: rounded margin to 12 decimals, strict >13.297415516625. Display score is sigmoid(margin), **uncalibrated** and liable to saturate; it is not probability of failure. Null decisions mean unassessed. Thermal NORMAL means no condition detected under this model, not certified health. Electrical warnings are independently configured at 110% phase current for 3 snapshots, 0.9 nominal phase voltage, and >40% current imbalance for 3 snapshots; they do not imply transformer damage.

Calibration is external to classifier training. Valid supplied calibration is asset-bound; separate independently verified healthy commissioning requires at least seven complete days/2016 five-minute readings. The original robust fitting function and bounds are retained. Inference never assumes its first week is healthy or calibrates on event-bearing input. A reference fallback uses the user's approximate pooled synthetic R05/R11 prototype (34.6685 K, 196.1612 min, −4 C, 0.242389). It is labeled REFERENCE_CALIBRATION, returns REDUCED confidence, and has no fabricated commissioning metrics. Bound-hit/near-bound limitations are explicit. Pooled parameters do not represent every asset and are not field commissioning.

The final reports record current 12-replay/48,384-row completeness, R05/R11 false alerts, 60 timestamp-aligned original thermal events, per-family detections, injection-start delays, reference-control RMSE, original-vs-hardened coverage and event changes, sensor/organizer matrix, strict JSON tests and fresh-environment tests. Prior 45/60 (75%) event detection is a historical regression reference. The new runtime's actual result is authoritative; compare it to the separately reproduced original runtime in reports. All failures and abstentions remain visible. The original model card reports 68.6% observable event recall on N replays and 63.3% on T stress replays, including known missed subtle thermal events; these acceptance figures have different data/calibration/method and are not interchangeable with the original 60-event deployment test.

Runtime safety retains original 24-hour warm-up and 12 clean snapshots after bad inputs. Missing driver hold is bounded at 60 minutes; longer driver loss and timestamp gaps invalidate state and restart warm-up. Oil spikes, implausible sensor values, expected-motion plateaus, frozen telemetry and strongly constant channels can cause abstention. These conservative rules reduce coverage and can miss genuine rapid faults. A constant plausible bias, incorrect units/sensor placement and subtle in-range faults cannot reliably be detected. Reference-calibration asset transfer is unverified. The package offers clean bounded row-by-row state, restart/save/load, batch parity and plain JSON, with no invented Health Index, risk, RUL or maintenance advice.

Organizer boundary accepts documented aliases, column reorder, extra columns, missing IDs, explicit unit conversion and timestamps; ambiguity and incompatible units fail. Bare aliases assume the documented V/A/C physical contract and are marked reduced confidence until explicit configuration. Full assessment requires genuine phase-neutral RMS VR/VY/VB, RMS IR/IY/IB, ambient and top oil on an exact five-minute grid. Additional loading is display-only and does not replace currents. Missing required signals and unsupported sampling are safely unassessed; wrong rating, missing timestamp schema, unknown units, numeric unconfigured dates or corrupt config/model cannot be assessed. No interpolated missing phases, ambient or oil are created.

Only rating 100 kVA is supported. No real-world accuracy, field certification, transformer protection suitability, predictive RUL accuracy or actual organizer-data performance is claimed. All SCADA and dry-run validation here are synthetic. Field use requires independently verified commissioning, meter/temperature validation, operating-domain checks and separate real asset evaluation. Classifier improvement and other ratings are outside this completed deployment task.
